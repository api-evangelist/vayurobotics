# Vayu Robotics

> Vayu Robotics is a Palo Alto, California robotics company building AI foundation models and sensing for autonomous mobile robots — Vayu Drive (an end-to-end learned, sim-first foundation model for mobility), Vayu Sense (a plenoptic sensor with embodied AI for high-resolution depth perception), a Vayu Sense Development Kit (bundled with NVIDIA Orin), and Vayu One (a low-cost autonomous delivery robot). Backed by Khosla Ventures. In September 2025 Vayu Robotics was acquired by Serve Robotics. Vayu Robotics publishes no public developer API, SDK, or developer portal.
